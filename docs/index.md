# Guns & Reloading
{: .fs-9 }

A self-hosted homelab app for tracking your firearm collection and reloading data — your own
server, your own data, nothing sent anywhere else.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

[View on GitHub](https://github.com/chelohomelab/guns-and-reloading){: .btn .btn-outline }

---

## What it does

- **Firearm inventory** — rifles, shotguns, handguns, and Thompson Center (receivers/barrels
  tracked independently), with photos and sale tracking
- **Optics** — a shared scope pool you can mount to any firearm or barrel
- **Reloading components** — powders, primers, bullets, and casings, with low-stock alerts
- **Ammo log** — factory and handloaded rounds, barcode-scannable
- **Range Session & Ladder Test** — chronograph data with automatic ES/SD, target-photo group
  measurement, and charge-weight load development
- **Reloading Data Center** — a built-in reference library across six manufacturers' published
  data
- **Wishlist**, **barcode scanner**, **offline-capable PWA** for phone/tablet use, and per-user
  feature preferences
